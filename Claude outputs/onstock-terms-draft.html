2. What OnStock is

OnStock is a free, publicly available information service. It shows market data for tokenized stocks (for example xStocks, Ondo Global Markets tokens, and bStocks) next to the price of the share each one tracks, and the difference between the two ("the gap"). You can keep watchlists and folders, set price and gap alerts, and read related news. You can use most of OnStock without an account.

OnStock is in beta. Features may change, pause, or stop at any time, and we may limit access.

TradingView's agreement (clause 2.4) allows its charts only in a free, public offering. If OnStock ever adds a paid tier, the charts cannot sit behind it. Should the terms promise the service is free, or only describe it as free today?

Rankings, sorts, gaps, alerts, and lessons describe market data. They do not tell you what to do. OnStock does not know your circumstances and does not consider them. 5. About tokenized stocks

A tokenized stock is issued by a company other than the one whose shares it tracks. Holding one is not the same as owning the share: it may give no voting or other shareholder rights, it depends on its issuer and on the blockchain it runs on, and its price can differ from the share's price. Issuers limit who may buy or redeem their tokens and where. OnStock does not issue, sell, or redeem any token, and is not affiliated with any issuer. Read the issuer's own documents before you act.

How much risk description belongs in the terms versus in the app itself. Keep this section factual and short, or replace it with a link to issuer documents.

7. Market data and accuracy

OnStock does not create market data. Prices, charts, volumes, liquidity figures, company information, and headlines come from third parties and may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Share prices are delayed and are the last close when the market is shut. Token prices refresh on a timer and may be stale. Figures that depend on an issuer's token-to-share ratio are only as current as our last reading of that ratio. Where OnStock cannot confirm a figure it labels it (for example "Ratio unverified" or "Gap not confirmed"), but it cannot catch every error. Do not rely on OnStock as your only source, and check prices with the venue before you trade.
