Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026 · applies to onstock.app, the Onstock iPhone app and their test versions. Onstock was called BlockWatch until 2026, and blockwatch.app now redirects here.

The short version

  • Onstock is a free tracker for tokenized stocks. You can use it without an account.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or share your data for advertising.
  • If you make an account, we keep your email, how you sign in, and the folders and alerts you save, so they follow you across devices.
  • Usage analytics use a random ID made on your device, never your name, email or account.
  • You can delete your account by emailing us. In-app deletion is coming soon. Below we list exactly what deleting removes and what it does not.
  • Onstock never holds your money or your wallet keys.

Who we are

Onstock is an independent app based in Arizona, United States ("we", "us"). It shows public market data about tokenized stocks. Onstock is not a broker, does not hold your funds, does not execute trades and does not give investment advice. For anything in this policy, email contact@onstock.app.

What we collect and why

If you just use the tracker (no account)

  • Usage analytics. We use PostHog, hosted in the United States, to count how the app is used, so we can tell what to build and fix. We send events such as: the app was opened; a search was made, including the text you typed (so please type tickers and company names, not personal information); a chart, share card or lesson was opened, and for which ticker; a folder or alert was created (not its name or contents); whether you allowed notifications; that someone signed in and which method was used; that you tapped out to a trading venue, which venue and for which ticker; and similar product events. Every event carries a random ID created on your device and says whether it came from the live app or a test version.
  • Browser and visit details. Each analytics event also carries your browser's user agent (a line of text that names your browser, its version, your operating system and your type of device), the address of the page where your visit started (without the part after a "?" or "#", which is where sign-in links carry their codes), the main address of the website that sent you to Onstock, if any, and the campaign tags (UTM parameters) in the link you followed, if it had them. Events never include your name, email, account, wallet address, folder names or holdings.
  • Holdings (website only). If you add holdings without an account, the tokens and amounts you type stay on your device. We do not receive them.
  • Feature switches. While the app is open, it sends the same random ID to PostHog about once a minute to check which features are switched on for your device.
  • Your IP address. Like any website, the services the app talks to see your IP address. We have set both PostHog and Sentry not to store IP addresses.
  • Error reports. When something breaks, the app sends an error report to Sentry: the error, where in the code it happened, your browser or device type and version, the page address (with any sign-in codes removed), and the last few things the app did before the error. Error reports do not include your account, your email or your IP address. We have set Sentry not to store IP addresses.
  • Server logs. Our host, Vercel, keeps standard request logs, such as IP address, time, the page or data requested (for example a stock ticker) and browser type.
  • Market data requests. To show prices, your device asks CoinGecko and Jupiter for token prices directly, so they see your IP address and which tokens are being priced. Company logos load directly from Finnhub's image servers, which see your IP address and which logos loaded. Stock quotes, price history, news headlines and Treasury yields come through our own servers from Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, GeckoTerminal, Google News and the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (its FRED service), so those providers see our server, not you. Tapping a headline opens the news site, which sees that visit.

If you joined the trading waitlist

The trading waitlist closed to new sign-ups on September 30, 2026. If you joined before then, we keep your email address and your place in line. We will send you one email only if in-app trading ever launches and is legally available where you live, and nothing else.

If you send feedback

We keep your message and, if you give one, a reply email address. If this device has a waitlist entry, the email field is filled in with it; you can clear it before sending. Nothing is sent until you tap send. We use feedback only to reply to you and to improve the app.

If you create an account (optional)

  • Email and sign-in. Your email address, and depending on how you sign in: a password (stored only as a scrambled hash by our sign-in provider, Supabase; we never see it), one-time sign-in links sent to your email, or Sign in with Apple.
  • Sign in with Apple. Apple gives us an email address, which may be a private relay address that forwards to you if you chose "Hide My Email". The first time you sign in with Apple, Apple may also share your name, which we keep with your account and show in your profile.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and passkeys (website only). If you add one, Supabase stores its public key, a name for it, and when it was created and last used. The private key and your fingerprint or face data never leave your device.
  • What you save. Your folders (their names and which assets are in them), your alert rules, and your holdings (which tokens and the amounts you typed in), so they sync across your devices.
  • Sign-in records. Supabase keeps records of sign-ins and sessions, which can include IP address, time and browser type, to keep accounts secure.

If you connect a wallet (website only)

If you connect a Phantom wallet, the app learns your wallet's public address and keeps it in the open page only. We do not send it to our servers or to analytics. Onstock cannot see your keys, move your funds or sign anything.

If you email us

Mail to contact@onstock.app or marcus@onstock.app passes through Cloudflare, which forwards it to our email inbox. We keep it for 24 months after our last message in that conversation, or sooner if you ask.

Notifications

Notifications are used only to deliver the price and drift alerts you set up yourself, and only if you grant permission when asked. They are made on your device while Onstock is open. We do not collect a notification token or send notifications from a server.

What stays on your device

The app saves working data in your browser's or phone's storage: price, logo and chart caches, your folders, watchlist and alert rules, your holdings, the history of alerts that fired, display and layout preferences, recently viewed assets, your waitlist entry (including its email) if you joined, the beta access code, the random analytics ID, where your current visit started, whether you have been offered Face ID or a passkey, your notification choice, and, when signed in, your session. None of it leaves your device except as this policy describes. Clearing the site's data, or deleting the app, removes it.

Cookies and tracking

Onstock does not set cookies and does not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels or session recording. It uses your device's storage as described above. Onstock does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals differently, because it does not track you across other sites. Links you tap and images from other sites (logos, news articles) are ordinary web requests to those sites, which follow their own policies.

Emails we send

  • Account emails: sign-in links, account confirmation and password resets.
  • Waitlist: one email, only if in-app trading ever launches and is legally available where you live.
  • Replies to your feedback or emails.

We do not send newsletters or marketing email. A sign-in link is a key to your account: anyone with access to your inbox could use one, so keep your email account secure.

How long we keep data

  • Account data (email, sign-in data, passkeys, folders, alerts, holdings): until you delete your account.
  • Waitlist entries: until we have sent the one email described above, or until September 30, 2028, whichever comes first. Then we delete them. You can ask us to remove yours sooner.
  • Feedback: 24 months after we receive it, or sooner if you ask.
  • Emails you send us: 24 months after our last message in that conversation, or sooner if you ask.
  • Analytics, error reports, server logs and email delivery logs: kept by PostHog, Sentry, Vercel and Resend under their own retention schedules. They are not linked to your account.
  • Backups: our database provider may keep backups for a limited time after data is deleted.

Deleting your data

Delete your account: email contact@onstock.app from your account's address and we will delete it. That permanently removes your account and your sign-in details, passkeys, synced folders, alerts and holdings. If your account email is confirmed, we also delete the waitlist entry and feedback sent with that same email address. To clear what is on your device, sign out and clear the site's data, or delete the app.

What account deletion does not delete: analytics tied to your device's random ID, error reports, server and email delivery logs, a waitlist entry or feedback sent with a different email address, emails you sent us, and backups until they expire. None of these is linked to your account. To ask us to remove what we can, email contact@onstock.app from the address in question.

Without an account: to remove your waitlist entry or your feedback, email contact@onstock.app from that address. To remove what is on your device, clear the site's data or delete the app.

Passkeys: your device keeps its copy of a passkey in its password manager. Remove it there after deleting your account.

Sign in with Apple: you can also remove Onstock at any time on iPhone in Settings, then your name, then Sign in with Apple.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or give you a copy. Email contact@onstock.app. We will answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking. We may need to confirm that you control the email address involved. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict some uses, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Where data is processed

Onstock and its providers are based in the United States, and your data is processed there. If you use Onstock from another country, your data is transferred to the United States.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, enforced). Secret keys and the ability to read stored emails stay on our servers and never reach your browser. Database rules limit account data to the signed-in owner. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Onstock is meant for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email contact@onstock.app and we will delete it.

Not investment advice

Onstock shows market data for information only. Prices may be delayed or wrong, and for thinly traded tokens they can be unreliable; the app flags those. Nothing in Onstock is investment advice, and Onstock is not a broker.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date, and note meaningful changes in the app. We will not use data we already hold in a new way that this policy did not allow without asking you first.
