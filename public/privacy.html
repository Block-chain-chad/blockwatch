Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026 · applies to blockwatch.app and its beta deployments

The short version

  • No ads, no selling or renting your data, ever.
  • No cookies. No camera, microphone, location, or push-notification access — those are disabled at the platform level.
  • You can use the whole tracker without an account. Accounts are optional and exist only to sync your folders and alerts.
  • We collect your email only when you give it to us (waitlist, account, or feedback), and we email you only for the reason you gave it.
  • Usage analytics are tied to a random device ID, never to your name or email.

Who we are

BlockWatch is an independently built tracker for tokenized stocks. It shows public market data; it does not hold funds, execute trades, or provide investment advice. For anything in this policy, contact contact@blockwatch.app.

What we collect, and why

  • Waitlist: your email address, stored with a signup position. Used to send one welcome email when you join and one email when trading opens — nothing else. Our systems are designed so the app itself can never read the waitlist back; only we can, in our database dashboard.
  • Account (optional): your email and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see your password). If you sign in, your watchlist, folder names, and price-alert settings sync to your account so they follow you across devices. Without an account, all of that stays on your device only.
  • Feedback: whatever you type in the feedback form, plus an optional reply email. If you've joined the waitlist on this device, forms pre-fill that address for convenience — you can clear it before sending, and nothing is submitted until you tap send.
  • Usage analytics: we use PostHog (US-hosted) to count a few events — app opens, waitlist joins (without the email), share-card taps, and searches, including the text of what was searched, so we can learn which assets people want. Events carry a random device ID stored on your device, never your name, email, or account. There is no click-tracking script, no session recording, and no advertising identifier. Like any web request, analytics requests expose your IP address to PostHog. One practical note: search is for tickers and company names — anything you type there is logged, so don't type personal information into it.

What stays on your device

The app keeps working data in your browser's local storage: price caches, your folders and watchlist, alert rules and fired-alert history, display preferences, recently viewed assets, your waitlist entry (including its email), and — when signed in — your session token. None of it leaves your device except as described in this policy. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Market data lookups

Showing you live prices means asking data providers for them, and those requests reveal ordinary web-request information:

  • CoinGecko (token prices): your browser calls CoinGecko directly, so CoinGecko sees your IP address and the token ids being refreshed — a standard set common to all users, plus the assets you watch, view, or set alerts on.
  • Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, Google News (stock quotes, charts, headlines): these requests go through our own servers, so those providers see our server's address, not yours. Two exceptions: company logo images load directly from Finnhub's image host (it sees your IP and which logos loaded), and tapping a headline follows a Google News link (Google and the news outlet see that visit, as with any link on the web).

Cookies

BlockWatch sets no cookies — first-party or third-party. All persistence uses local storage on your device. External images and links you tap (logos, news articles) are ordinary web requests to other sites, which behave per those sites' own policies.

Emails we send

Only transactional ones: the waitlist welcome, sign-in links, account confirmation, and password resets. No newsletters, no marketing. A note worth knowing: sign-in links are literal keys to your account — anyone with access to your inbox could use one, so keep your email account secure.

Retention and your choices

  • Waitlist, feedback, and account data are kept until you ask us to remove them.
  • To delete anything — your waitlist entry, feedback, or entire account — email contact@blockwatch.app from the address in question and we'll remove it.
  • Signing out removes your session from the device; clearing site data removes everything stored locally.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS, enforced). API keys and the ability to read stored emails live server-side only — they are never shipped to your browser. Database access rules restrict account data to the signed-in owner.

Children

BlockWatch is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Not investment advice

Prices shown are informational and may be delayed or, for thinly traded tokens, unreliable — the app flags those. Nothing in BlockWatch is investment advice.

Changes

If our practices change, this page and its date change with them. Meaningful changes will be noted in the app.
